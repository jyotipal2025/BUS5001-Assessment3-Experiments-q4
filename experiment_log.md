# Q4 Experiment Log – NotebookLM Evaluation

## Purpose
The aim of this experiment was to evaluate NotebookLM as a university study assistant for BUS5001 Cloud Platforms and Analytics.

## Source Used
BUS5001 lecture material was uploaded into NotebookLM and used as the source for all experiments.

## Features Tested
- source-grounded question answering
- study guide and revision question generation
- mind map
- interactive quiz
- Audio Overview

## Experiment 1 – Study Guide and Revision Questions
NotebookLM generated revision questions and short model answers from the uploaded lecture material.

### Observation
The output was relevant to the lecture content and useful for exam preparation. Citations were shown beside many answers, which made it easier to trace information back to the source.

### Limitation
The quality of the output depends on the quality and completeness of the uploaded source. Important detail may also be simplified during summarisation.

---

## Experiment 2 – Mind Map
NotebookLM generated a visual mind map covering major Cloud Platforms topics.

### Observation
The mind map was useful for organising topics and showing relationships between areas such as cloud storage, databases, data architecture and conversational AI.

### Limitation
The mind map gives a simplified overview and may not capture every detail required for assessment preparation.

---

## Experiment 3 – Interactive Quiz
NotebookLM generated multiple-choice questions from the source material.

### Observation
The quiz supported active recall and was useful for testing understanding of concepts such as database types and cloud architecture.

### Limitation
Generated questions should still be checked against the lecture material because AI-generated questions may simplify or interpret content differently.

---

## Experiment 4 – Audio Overview
NotebookLM generated an Audio Overview from the uploaded lecture source.

### Observation
The audio format was useful for revision when reading was not convenient. It provided another way to review lecture content.

### Limitation
Audio summaries may omit technical detail or reduce complex concepts to simpler explanations.

## Overall Evaluation
NotebookLM was useful as a study-support tool because it transformed the same lecture source into several different learning formats.

The strongest feature was source grounding because answers could be checked against the uploaded material.

However, source grounding does not guarantee complete accuracy. If the source is incomplete or contains an error, NotebookLM may repeat that problem.

NotebookLM should therefore be used as a revision and study-support tool rather than as a replacement for lectures, textbooks or independent academic judgement.

## Responsible Use
Students should:
- check important claims against the original source
- avoid uploading confidential or restricted material unless permitted
- follow university academic integrity requirements
- avoid over-relying on AI-generated summaries
